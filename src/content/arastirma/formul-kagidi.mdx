## 1 · İskonto faktörü ve kotasyon

**Üç kotasyon, tek DF**

$$
DF = \frac{1}{1 + z\,t} \quad \text{(simple)}, \qquad DF = (1+z)^{-t} \quad \text{(yıllık bileşik)}, \qquad DF = e^{-z\,t} \quad \text{(sürekli)}
$$

**Konvansiyon dönüşümü**

$$
z_c = -\frac{\ln DF}{t}, \qquad z_{simple} = \frac{1/DF - 1}{t}, \qquad z_{ann} = DF^{-1/t} - 1
$$

**Gün bazlı yazım**

$$
DF = \frac{1}{1 + R_s\cdot d/365}, \qquad DF = \left(1+\frac{R_g}{365}\right)^{-d}, \qquad DF = e^{-R_c\, T}
$$

**Sürekli limit**

$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{z}{n}\right)^{n t} = e^{z\,t}
$$

**Bugünkü değer**

$$
PV = \sum_i CF_i \cdot DF_i
$$

## 2 · Forward ve FRA

**İki DF'den forward (teleskop)**

$$
DF(T_2) = DF(T_1)\cdot\left(1+\frac{F}{365}\right)^{-(d_2-d_1)}
\;\Rightarrow\;
F[T_1,T_2] = 365\left[\left(\frac{DF(T_1)}{DF(T_2)}\right)^{\frac{1}{d_2-d_1}}-1\right]
$$

**Tarihten tarihe**

$$
F[d_1,d_2] = 365\left[\left(\frac{DF(d_1)}{DF(d_2)}\right)^{\frac{1}{d_2-d_1}}-1\right]
$$

**Zero'lardan forward (sürekli/doğrusal)**

$$
f(t_1, t_2) = \frac{z_2 t_2 - z_1 t_1}{t_2 - t_1}
$$

## 3 · Par swap ve bootstrap

**Par denklemi**

$$
R\,\tau \sum_{i=1}^{N} DF_i = 1 - DF_N
$$

**Değişken bacak teleskobu**

$$
PV_{float} = \sum_i \tau_i F_i\, DF_i = \sum_i\left(DF_{i-1}-DF_i\right) = DF_0 - DF_N = 1 - DF_N
$$

**Par oranı (genel)**

$$
R = \frac{DF(spot) - DF(T)}{\sum_i \tau_i\, DF(t_i)}, \qquad S_N = \frac{1 - DF_N}{\sum_i \tau_i DF_i}
$$

**K = 1 · kapalı form** (yeni pillar bir ödeme dönemi ilerideyse)

$$
DF_N = \frac{1 - R\,\tau\, S_{prev}}{1 + R\,\tau}, \qquad S_{prev} = \sum_{i=1}^{N-1} DF_i
$$

**K = 2 · ikinci derece** ($y = DF_N^{1/2}$, $a = DF_{prev}$)

$$
(1+R\tau)\,y^2 + R\tau\sqrt{a}\; y + \left(R\tau S_{prev} - 1\right) = 0
$$

**K ≥ 2 · Newton-Raphson** ($u = (DF_N/a)^{1/K}$)

$$
g(u) = (1+R\tau)\,u^K + \sum_{j=1}^{K-1} R\tau\, a^{\frac{K-j}{K}}\, u^{\,j} + \left(R\tau S_{prev}-1\right) = 0
$$

**Öz-tutarlılık sağlaması** — bootstrap'lanmış eğri girdiyi geri vermeli

$$
R_{implied} = \frac{1-DF_N}{\tau \sum_i DF_i} \;\overset{!}{=}\; R_{girdi}
$$

## 4 · Forward starting swap

**DF'den**

$$
R_{fwd}(t_1,t_2) = \frac{DF(t_1)-DF(t_2)}{A_{fwd}}, \qquad A_{fwd} = \sum_{t_i\in(t_1,t_2]} \tau_i DF(t_i)
$$

**Par oranlardan (annuity ağırlıklı)**

$$
R_{fwd} = \frac{R_{t_2}A(t_2) - R_{t_1}A(t_1)}{A(t_2)-A(t_1)}
$$

**Bacak ayrıştırması**

$$
\text{receive } 1y1y \approx \text{receive } 2y + \text{pay } 1y \quad (\text{DV01-nötr nominallerle})
$$

## 5 · İnterpolasyon

**Zero × vade üzerinde doğrusal**

$$
f(t_1, t_2) = \frac{z_2 t_2 - z_1 t_1}{t_2 - t_1}
$$

**DF üzerinde log-doğrusal (kırık tarih)**

$$
DF(t) = DF(t_1)^{\,w} \times DF(t_2)^{\,1-w}, \qquad w = \frac{t_2 - t}{t_2 - t_1}
$$

## 6 · Tahvil fiyatlama

**İşlemiş faiz ve kirli fiyat**

$$
AI = \frac{C}{m}\cdot\frac{\text{geçen gün}}{\text{dönem günü}}, \qquad DP = P_{temiz} + AI
$$

**İlk kupon kesri ve üs**

$$
w = 1 - \frac{\text{geçen gün}}{\text{dönem günü}} = \frac{\text{ilk kupona kalan gün}}{\text{dönem günü}}
\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad
\text{üs}_i = (i-1) + w
$$

**Fiyat**

$$
DP = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{CF_i}{(1+r)^{\,\text{üs}_i}}, \qquad r = \frac{y}{m}
$$

**Durasyon** — $D_{Mac}$'ın birimi konvansiyonun parçasıdır

$$
D_{mod} = -\frac{1}{P}\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}
$$

$$
D_{Mac}^{\text{yıl}} = \frac{\sum_i t_i \cdot CF_i \cdot DF_i}{P} \;\Rightarrow\; D_{mod} = \frac{D_{Mac}^{\text{yıl}}}{1+r}
$$

$$
D_{Mac}^{\text{dönem}} = \frac{\sum_i \text{üs}_i \cdot BD_i}{DP} \;\Rightarrow\; D_{mod} = \frac{D_{Mac}^{\text{dönem}}}{1+r}\Big/ m
$$

**DV01**

$$
DV01 = D_{mod} \cdot P \cdot 10^{-4}
$$

**Konveksite (bump)**

$$
C = \frac{P_{+} + P_{-} - 2P}{P\,(\Delta y)^2}
$$

## 7 · DV01 konvansiyonları

**DF'nin orana türevi**

$$
\text{Sürekli:} \ \frac{\partial DF}{\partial z} = -t\,DF, \qquad
\text{Simple:} \ \frac{\partial DF}{\partial z} = -t\,DF^{2}, \qquad
\text{Yıllık:} \ \frac{\partial DF}{\partial z} = -\frac{t}{1+z}\,DF
$$

**Türev çekirdeği (simple)**

$$
\frac{dDF}{dr} = -\frac{\tau}{(1 + r\tau)^2} = -\tau \cdot DF^2
$$

**Tek bullet DV01**

$$
DV01_{cont} = N\,t\,DF \times 10^{-4}, \qquad DV01_{simple} = N\,t\,DF^{2} \times 10^{-4}, \qquad DV01_{ann} = N\,\frac{t}{1+z}\,DF \times 10^{-4}
$$

**Oran kimliği**

$$
\frac{DV01_{cont}}{DV01_{simple}} = \frac{1}{DF} = 1 + z_{simple}\,t
$$

**Braket (sağlama)**

$$
N\,t\,DF^{2} \times 10^{-4} \;\le\; DV01 \;\le\; N\,t\,DF \times 10^{-4}
$$

**Merkezi fark**

$$
DV01_{central} = \frac{PV(r + 1\,\text{bp}) - PV(r - 1\,\text{bp})}{2}
$$

**Nominal geri-çözme**

$$
N_{ima} = \frac{DV01}{t \cdot DF \times 10^{-4}} \qquad \text{(sürekli tanımda)}
$$

**Konveksite (tek bullet, sürekli)**

$$
C = N\,t^{2}\,DF \times 10^{-8}, \qquad \Delta PV_{2.\text{derece}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,C\,(\Delta r)^{2}
$$

## 8 · Swap riski ve hedge

**Annuity ve PV01**

$$
A = \sum_i \tau_i \cdot DF_i, \qquad PV01 = A \cdot N \times 10^{-4}
$$

**Swap MtM**

$$
PV \approx (K - S_{piyasa})\cdot A\cdot N, \qquad DV01 \approx A\cdot N \times 10^{-4}
$$

**Hedge nominali**

$$
N_{OIS} = \frac{DV01_{fwd}}{PV01_{OIS,\,\text{birim}}}, \qquad
N_{swap} = N_{tahvil} \cdot \frac{DV01_{tahvil}}{DV01_{swap}}
$$

**Hedge nominali — DF tabanlı eşdeğer**

$$
N_{OIS} = \frac{DV01_{TRY}}{\tau_{TRY} \times DF_{TRY} \times 10^{-4}}
$$

**Fly ağırlığı (50:50)**

$$
N_{\text{kanat}} = 0{,}5\cdot N_{\text{gövde}}\cdot
\frac{\text{DV01}_{\text{gövde}}}{\text{DV01}_{\text{kanat}}}
$$

**Boyutlandırma tavanı**

$$
\text{Nosyonel} \le \min\left\{
\frac{\text{Risk bütçesi}}{\text{stop mesafesi}\cdot \text{DV01}_{\text{birim}}},\;
\text{Çıkış klipi},\;
\text{Limit alanı}
\right\}
$$

## 9 · OIS ve TLREF

**Geriye dönük bileşikleme**

$$
\text{Kupon} = \prod_i \left(1 + TLREF_i \cdot \frac{n_i}{365}\right) - 1
$$

**Yıllıklandırılmış eşdeğer**

$$
F = \left[\prod_i \left(1 + r_i\cdot\frac{d_i}{365}\right) - 1\right]\cdot\frac{365}{D}
$$

**Günlük patika**

$$
\text{Accrual}_n = 1+\frac{r_n}{365},
\qquad
CumDF_n = \frac{CumDF_{n-1}}{\text{Accrual}_n}
$$

**Toplantı bazlı ima (MPC implied)**

$$
MPC_k = 365\left[\left(\frac{DF(\text{eff}_k)}{DF(\text{eff}_{k+1})}\right)^{1/\text{gün}}-1\right], \qquad G_k = MPC_{k+1} - MPC_k
$$

**Gün ağırlıklı strip kotasyonu**

$$
\text{Kotasyon}_D \approx \frac{\sum_k \text{gün}_k\cdot \text{seviye}_k}{D},
\qquad
\text{seviye}_k = \text{TLREF}_0 + \sum_{j\le k}\Delta_j
$$

**Taşıma bileşenleri**

$$
\text{fixing carry} \approx (K - \overline{TLREF})\cdot\frac{h}{365}\cdot N, \qquad
\text{eğri-sabit taşıma} = \text{fixing carry} + \text{rolldown}
$$

## 10 · FX forward ve çapraz kur riski

**CIP**

$$
F = S \cdot \frac{1 + r_{TRY}\,t}{1 + r_{USD}\,t} = S \cdot \frac{DF_{USD}}{DF_{TRY}}
$$

**İki bacaklı PV**

$$
PV = N_{USD} \times S \times DF_{USD} \;-\; N_{TRY} \times DF_{TRY} \qquad \text{(TRY cinsinden)}
$$

**Tek terimli hâli**

$$
V_{TRY} = N_{USD} \cdot (F - K) \cdot DF_{TRY}
$$

**Bacak DV01'leri (simple kotasyon)**

$$
DV01_{USD} = -\,N_{USD} \times S \times \tau_{USD} \times DF_{USD}^{2} \times 10^{-4}, \qquad
DV01_{TRY} = +\,N_{TRY} \times \tau_{TRY} \times DF_{TRY}^{2} \times 10^{-4}
$$

**TRY DV01 tabanı** — $F$ sadeleşir, taban $K$'dir

$$
\frac{\partial V}{\partial r_{TRY}} = -\,N_{USD} \cdot K \cdot DF_{TRY}'
$$

## 11 · Asset swap

**ASW marjı**

$$
ASW = y_{tahvil} - R^{OIS}_{par}(T_{tahvil})
$$

**Net taşıma**

$$
\text{net carry (bp/yıl)} = ASW - \text{repo bazı}
$$

**Eğri-sabit taşıma (spread için)**

$$
\text{eğri-sabit taşıma}(h) = \text{spot spread} - \text{forward spread}(h)
$$

## 12 · Swaption

**Dayanak forward swap oranı**

$$
F = \frac{DF(T_{exp}) - DF(T_{mat})}{A}, \qquad A = \sum \tau_i DF(t_i) \;\; (T_{exp} < t_i \le T_{mat})
$$

**Black (lognormal)**

$$
d_1 = \frac{\ln(F/K) + \sigma^2 T/2}{\sigma\sqrt{T}},\quad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T}
$$

$$
\text{Payer} = A\left[F\,N(d_1) - K\,N(d_2)\right],
\qquad
\text{Receiver} = A\left[K\,N(-d_2) - F\,N(-d_1)\right]
$$

**Bachelier (normal)**

$$
d = \frac{F-K}{\sigma_N\sqrt{T}},\qquad
\text{Payer} = A\left[(F-K)N(d) + \sigma_N\sqrt{T}\,n(d)\right],
\qquad \sigma_N \approx \sigma_{Black}\times F
$$

**Parite**

$$
\text{Payer} - \text{Receiver} = A\,(F-K)\times N
$$

**Implied vol (Newton)**

$$
\sigma_{n+1} = \sigma_n - \frac{P(\sigma_n) - P_{mkt}}{\text{Vega}(\sigma_n)}
$$

## 13 · FX opsiyon

**Forward ve Garman-Kohlhagen**

$$
F = S \, e^{(r_d - r_f)\,T}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Call} &= e^{-r_d T}\left[\,F\,N(d_1) - K\,N(d_2)\,\right] \\[4pt]
\text{Put}  &= e^{-r_d T}\left[\,K\,N(-d_2) - F\,N(-d_1)\,\right] \\[4pt]
d_1 &= \frac{\ln(F/K) + \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma^2 T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}}\,, \qquad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Birinci derece Greeks**

$$
\Delta_{spot} = e^{-r_f T}\cdot N(d_1), \qquad
\Gamma = \frac{e^{-r_f T}\cdot n(d_1)}{S \cdot \sigma \cdot \sqrt{T}}, \qquad
\text{Vega} = S \cdot e^{-r_f T} \cdot n(d_1) \cdot \sqrt{T}
$$

**Cash gamma** — %1 spot hareketi için delta değişimi

$$
\Gamma_{cash} = \Gamma \times S \times 1\% \times N
$$

**İkinci derece Greeks**

$$
\text{Vanna} = -e^{-r_f T} \cdot n(d_1) \cdot \frac{d_2}{\sigma}, \qquad
\text{Volga} = \text{Vega} \cdot \frac{d_1 \, d_2}{\sigma}
$$

**Bump karşılığı**

$$
\text{Vanna} \approx \frac{\text{Vega}(S+\Delta S) - \text{Vega}(S-\Delta S)}{2\,\Delta S}
\qquad
\text{Volga} \approx \frac{\text{Vega}(\sigma+\Delta\sigma) - \text{Vega}(\sigma-\Delta\sigma)}{2\,\Delta\sigma}
$$

**P&L ayrıştırması**

$$
\Delta V \;\approx\; \Delta\cdot\Delta S
\;+\; \tfrac12\,\Gamma\,(\Delta S)^2
\;+\; \mathrm{Vega}\cdot\Delta\sigma
\;+\; \Theta\cdot\Delta t
\;+\; \mathrm{Vanna}\cdot\Delta S\cdot\Delta\sigma
\;+\; \tfrac12\,\mathrm{Volga}\,(\Delta\sigma)^2
\;+\; \mathrm{Residual}
$$

**Gamma P&L ve başabaş hareket**

$$
\text{Günlük gamma P\&L} \;\approx\; \tfrac{1}{2}\,\Gamma_{cash}\,(\Delta S)^2, \qquad
\Delta S_{BE} \;=\; \sigma\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{252}}
$$

**Vade kovasına dağıtım (off-grid)**

$$
w_{1} = \frac{T_{2} - T}{T_{2} - T_{1}}, \qquad w_{2} = 1 - w_{1}
$$

**Smile kotasyonu**

$$
\sigma_{25C} = \sigma_{ATM} + BF_{25} + \tfrac{RR_{25}}{2},\qquad \sigma_{25P} = \sigma_{ATM} + BF_{25} - \tfrac{RR_{25}}{2}
$$

$$
RR = \sigma_C - \sigma_P, \qquad BF = \tfrac{\sigma_C + \sigma_P}{2} - \sigma_{ATM}
$$

**Risk-nötr yoğunluk (Breeden-Litzenberger)**

$$
\varphi(K) \;=\; e^{r_d T}\,\frac{\partial^2 C}{\partial K^2}
$$

**Digital ve skew düzeltmesi**

$$
\text{Digital} = -\frac{\partial C}{\partial K} = e^{-r_d T}\,N(d_2) - \text{Vega}(K)\cdot\frac{d\sigma}{dK}
$$

**Çapraz kur**

$$
S_{EURTRY} = S_{EURUSD}\times S_{USDTRY}, \qquad
\sigma^2_{EURTRY} = \sigma^2_{EURUSD} + \sigma^2_{USDTRY} + 2\,\rho\,\sigma_{EURUSD}\,\sigma_{USDTRY}
$$

**Çapraz delta**

$$
\frac{\partial V}{\partial S_{EURUSD}} = \Delta_{EURTRY}\times S_{USDTRY}
\qquad
\frac{\partial V}{\partial S_{USDTRY}} = \Delta_{EURTRY}\times S_{EURUSD}
$$

**Çapraz gamma**

$$
\Gamma_{\times} = \frac{V(S_1^{\uparrow},S_2^{\uparrow}) - V(S_1^{\uparrow},S_2^{\downarrow}) - V(S_1^{\downarrow},S_2^{\uparrow}) + V(S_1^{\downarrow},S_2^{\downarrow})}{4\,\Delta S_1\,\Delta S_2}
$$

**Vega ayrıştırması**

$$
\frac{\partial \sigma_{ET}}{\partial \sigma_{EU}} = \frac{\sigma_{EU} + \rho\,\sigma_{UT}}{\sigma_{ET}}, \qquad
\frac{\partial \sigma_{ET}}{\partial \rho} = \frac{\sigma_{EU}\,\sigma_{UT}}{\sigma_{ET}}
$$

## 14 · Regresyon tabanlı hedge

**Beta**

$$
\beta = \frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y)}{\operatorname{Var}(X)} = \rho \cdot \frac{\sigma_Y}{\sigma_X}, \qquad Y_t = \beta \, X_t + \alpha + \varepsilon_t
$$

**Ters regresyon kimliği**

$$
\beta_{Y|X} \times \beta_{X|Y} = R^2
$$

**Blume düzeltmesi**

$$
\beta_{adj} = 0{,}67 \times \beta_{raw} + 0{,}33 \times 1{,}0
$$

**Artık ölçüleri**

$$
SEE = \sigma_\varepsilon = \sigma_Y \cdot \sqrt{1 - R^2}, \qquad
SE(\beta) = \frac{SEE}{\sigma_X \cdot \sqrt{N - 1}}
$$

**Anlamlılık**

$$
t = \frac{\beta}{SE(\beta)} = \frac{R \cdot \sqrt{N - 2}}{\sqrt{1 - R^2}}, \qquad
\beta \pm 1{,}96 \times SE(\beta)
$$

**Alfa anlamlılığı**

$$
t(\alpha) = \frac{\alpha}{SE(\alpha)}
$$

**Kalan oynaklık ve pozisyon tavanı**

$$
\frac{\text{Kalan vol}}{\text{Orijinal vol}} = \sqrt{1 - R^2}, \qquad
V_{\max} = \frac{\text{risk bütçesi}}{SEE}
$$

**Sapma ve bant**

$$
\text{Last T-Value} = \frac{\text{son artık}}{SEE}, \qquad
\text{Bant}(1\sigma) \approx \sqrt{SEE^2 + (SE(\beta)\cdot\Delta X)^2}
$$

**Ortalamaya dönüş**

$$
\Delta z(t) = c + \varphi\cdot z(t-1) + u(t), \qquad \text{Half-life} = \frac{-\ln 2}{\ln(1+\varphi)} \quad (\varphi < 0)
$$

**Tahvil eşdeğeri**

$$
\Delta P_{\%} \approx -D_{mod} \cdot \Delta y
$$

**İma edilen hareket ve sapma**

$$
\Delta Y_{\text{ima}} = \beta \cdot \Delta X + \alpha, \qquad \text{gap} = \Delta Y_{\text{gerçek}} - \Delta Y_{\text{ima}}, \qquad \text{gap}(\sigma) = \frac{\text{gap}}{SEE}
$$

**Çok dönemli**

$$
\Delta Y_{\text{ima}}^{\text{kum}} \approx \beta \cdot \Delta X_{\text{kum}} + k \cdot \alpha, \qquad \sigma_{\text{kum}} = \sqrt{k}\cdot SEE
$$

**Spread P&L kimliği**

$$
\text{P\&L} = V_Y\cdot\Delta Y\% - V_X\cdot\Delta X\% = V_Y\cdot(\Delta Y\% - \beta\cdot\Delta X\%) = V_Y\cdot\frac{\alpha+\varepsilon}{100}
$$

**Ödül/risk (half-life $h$ ile)**

$$
\frac{\text{Reward}}{\text{Risk}} \approx \frac{1{,}5\cdot SEE}{SEE\cdot\sqrt{2h}} = \frac{1{,}5}{\sqrt{2h}}
$$
